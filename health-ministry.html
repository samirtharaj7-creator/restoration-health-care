Equipping people to help one another.

The Rocky Mountain Conference Health Ministries department supports local churches and communities in personal health and trains members to serve as health ministers. The approach is simple: Christ-centered support, never criticism or comparison.

Ways to grow, and to serve.

Whether you want to improve your own health or help someone else improve theirs, there's a place to start.
