Learn to walk with others toward health.

LIFT is a free training from the Rocky Mountain Conference that teaches you how to support someone through real lifestyle change. Not by lecturing, but by listening first.

Four words. One way of helping.

Each step builds on the one before it, so the person you're helping stays in charge of their own change.

  1. LListen
  2. IInspire
  3. FFacilitate
  4. TTransform

Learn it yourself, or teach a group.

Work through LIFT on your own at your own pace, or use the train-the-trainer option to lead a live session for your church or community.
